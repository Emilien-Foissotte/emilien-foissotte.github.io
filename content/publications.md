# 2022

## May

Operationnal Research - SESAR Exercice

My main objective was to develop 2 tools using live feed of Orly airport :

- A runway inspection tool (p.43-44 in validation report) in order to estimate the environmental
  impact of operations
  ![Runway Inspection Tool](/img/publications/valr_rwy_inspection.png#center)

- A fuel-saving trajectories tool (p.44-45 in validation report)
  ![Trajectories Recommendation](/img/publications/valr_traj_reco.png#center)
  ![Speed Recommendation](/img/publications/valr_spd_reco.png#center)

A complete overview of theses studies and tool have been done by EUROCONTROL and ADP executives team
in the validation report. It's available on CORDIS, and a copy is here :

- [Validation Report](https://emilienfoissotte.fr/public/sharefolder/D3.3.040%20SESAR%20PJ.04-W2%2029.3%20VALR.pdf)
